What each layer is allowed to do

    
The split is not cosmetic — it is what keeps the fact re-runnable.


    
Bronze — nothing

    

      Already registered in models/bronze/olxuz_source.yml. Both
      olxuz_paidads_user_payments and olxuz_paidads_indexes are listed today, just
      undocumented — so this work adds descriptions, not sources.
    


    
Silver — shape, never meaning

    

      Dedup, last-write-wins, casts, timezone. The sign flip on price and dropping top-up rows
      belong here because they correct the source's shape, not because they decide anything. No category
      logic, no VAT, no revenue buckets.
    


    
Gold — the business rules, once

    

      fct_olxuz_payments is where product_type becomes
      revenue_stream, the finance category resolves, and net comes off gross. One row per
      payment, so a distinct count of paying listers stays correct at any grain — the reason this is a fact
      and not a daily aggregate.
    

  


  

    
Three things to settle before building


    
The name collides with the ads convention

    

      Ads use stg_olxuz_ads_log for the CDC log and stg_olxuz_ads for current
      state. Payments took the unsuffixed name for the log, and olxuz_source.yml
      states the contract outright: "stg_olxuz_payments stays log-shaped." So the current-state
      model cannot claim the name it wants. stg_olxuz_payments_current is the least-bad
      option; renaming the existing model is cleaner but breaks its readers.
    


    
The metric catalogue entry is wrong today

    

      _metrics.yml declares revenue_total as unit: "RUB" with
      source_marts: ["gold.fct_orders (TBD)"]. Both need correcting — and someone has to
      decide whether the metric reports UZS or converts to USD, since eu_bi and FPA both report USD.
    


    
Category is only trustworthy for recent months

    

      Do not ship a category split yet
      

        Packet purchases carry no id_ad and cannot be attributed — 10.3% of August 2026
        revenue, all of it NNL. A further 2.2% has a category_id missing from
        dim_categories, because the bronze category ingest stops at id 2099.
      

      

        Backwards it collapses: June 2021 lands 86.5% unattributed, because
        stg_olxuz_ads is current state and those ads are long deleted. Revenue totals
        are right at any date. The split is not.
      

    

    

      Which makes the build order: seeds first, so the finance map is reviewable on its own; then the two
      staging models; then the fact with an explicit unattributed bucket rather than a silent
      misallocation; then the metric.
